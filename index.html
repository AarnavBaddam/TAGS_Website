About TAGS

Our Roots Run Deep, Our Community Grows Forward

TAGS was founded by Telangana families in St. Louis who wanted a place where language, values, and traditions stayed alive across generations.

From Ugadi and Bathukamma to youth showcases and volunteer drives, we create shared spaces where culture feels lived, not archived.

Whether you are newly arrived or long-settled in Missouri, TAGS helps build friendships, practical support, and a sense of belonging rooted in home.

Today, TAGS connects 500+ families, hosts 20+ annual programs, and has served the region for 15+ years.
